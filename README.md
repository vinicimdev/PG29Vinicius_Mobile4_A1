# Super Amazing Ball Game

Simple ball game made with Unity 3D + Accelerometer + Camera.

## How to Play

Download the .zip of this repo, extract the folder, and open the folder with Unity Hub (version 6000.3.8f1). 
Then, download Unity Remote 5 on your phone from the Play Store, connect your phone to your computer, follow Unity Remote 5
instructions, and click the Play button in Unity.

After that, tilt your phone to move the ball around the arena. The goal is to collect as many coins as you can. 
Each coin will provide 10 points. The game ends when the timer runs out. Try beating your high score!

## Features

- Accelerometer-based control
- Camera so you can play wherever you want while feeling super immersed in the game
- Score system based on how many coins you collect
- Timer
- Full menu flow, all in the same scene to avoid scene loading
